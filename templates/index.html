PitaJell

Turning dragon fruit peel waste into natural pectin jelly — an edible waste treatment solution.

01 — CONTEXT

Background

Dragon fruit peel waste is often discarded, even though it accounts for 25–45% of the total fruit weight. The PitaJell project serves as a waste treatment solution to process this waste into a highly nutritious jelly product.

02 — GOALS

Objectives

To reduce organic dragon fruit peel waste in the school environment, optimize the extraction of natural pectin and antioxidants, and discover the jelly recipe with the most optimal texture score.
