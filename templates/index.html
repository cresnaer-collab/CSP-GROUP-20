PitaJell

Turning dragon fruit peel waste into natural pectin jelly — an edible waste treatment solution.

01 — Context

Background

Dragon fruit peel waste is often discarded, even though it accounts for 25–45% of the total fruit weight. The PitaJell project serves as a waste treatment solution to process this waste into a highly nutritious jelly product.

02 — Goals

Objectives

To reduce organic dragon fruit peel waste in the school environment, optimize the extraction of natural pectin and antioxidants, and discover the jelly recipe with the most optimal texture score.

03 — Materials

About Ingredients

9–17%

Natural Pectin

Percentage of pectin from dry weight of dragon fruit peel — sufficient to form jelly texture without added gelatin.

Antioxidants

Dragon fruit peel is rich in antioxidant compounds (including betacyanin) that help protect body cells from damage.

25–45%

Wasted Weight

Proportion of peel weight relative to total fruit usually discarded — this is the waste PitaJell reprocesses.

04 — Composition

Nutritional Content

PitaJell is rich in natural dietary fiber, vitamin C, and betacyanin pigments that act as natural antioxidants against free radicals without artificial food coloring.
