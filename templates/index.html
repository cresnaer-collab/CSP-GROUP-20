Contents of a Dragon Fruit & Health Benefits

The Dragon fruit (Hylocereus spp.) has lots of nutritious components rich in bioactive phytochemical compounds and health benefits:

Betalains

Nitrogen-containing water-soluble pigments responsible for the deep pink-violet hue of pitaya. Provide strong cellular protection and inflammation support.

Flavonoids

Secondary plant metabolites that scavenge free radicals and support vascular cell health. Excellent for cellular defense and radical neutralization.

Phenolic Acids

Potent hydroxylated compounds present in peel fiber that neutralize reactive oxygen species. Support oxidative balance and immunity boosting.

Anthocyanins

Vibrant plant pigments demonstrating natural antimicrobial and immunomodulatory activity. Active in microbial defense and immunity support.

Biology

Dragon fruit contains beneficial compounds like flavonoids, phenolic acids, betalains, and anthocyanins, which act as antioxidants, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial agents. Using both the flesh and skin also helps reduce biological waste while still providing nutritional value, such as fiber.

Chemistry

During the cooking process, ingredients undergo chemical interactions. Gelatin forms a gel structure when cooled, giving the candy its chewy texture. Sugar dissolves and helps with preservation and taste, while citric acid adds sourness and controls the pH, affecting stability and flavor.

Step-by-Step Methodology

1

Preparation

Thoroughly wash the dragon fruit. Separate the pink skin and the red flesh, ensuring a 1:1 ratio by weight. Clean the outer skin of any rough scales or blemishes.

→
2

Steaming the Skin

Place the dragon fruit skin into the steamer. Steam until the skin is softened. This helps reduce bitterness and soften the tough outer layer.

↓
3

Blending

Transfer the softened skin and fresh dragon fruit flesh into the blender. Blend until completely smooth and uniform.

←
4

Combining Ingredients

Pour the blended mixture into the pot. Add 1 tablespoon gelatin, 6 tablespoons sugar, 1 teaspoon cornstarch, and 1 teaspoon citric acid. Mix thoroughly using a spoon or spatula.

↓
5

Cooking and Dissolving

Heat over low-to-medium heat. Stir continuously to prevent burning and ensure the gelatin and sugar fully dissolve into a smooth, thick liquid.

→
6

Molding

Pour the hot mixture evenly into a rectangular metal mold or any preferred container.

↓
7

Setting

Let it cool at room temperature, then refrigerate until completely firm and set.

←
8

Cutting and Finishing

Remove from the mold and place on a clean surface. Use a knife to cut into neat cubes or rectangular pieces.
