Background

In SMA Pradita Dirgantara, students and citizens mostly eat from rukan. But most of the time, the kitchen won't use every single ingredient they have, hence waste from ingredients begins to pile up, creating a problem needing to be solved.

Dragon fruit peel accounts for approximately 30-35% of the fruit's total weight and is commonly discarded as organic waste. (Nurhafsah et al., 2023)

STEM Framework

Cross-Disciplinary Integration in Food Innovation

S

Science

  • Gelatin forms a gel matrix when cooled, creating chewy texture.
  • Sugar acts as preservative and flavor enhancer.
  • Dragon fruit is rich in vital minerals such as calcium, magnesium, potassium, iron, phosphorus, and sodium.
  • Ginger is rich in various chemical constituents, including phenolic compounds, terpenes, polysaccharides, lipids, organic acids, and raw fibers.
T

Technology

  • Develop website to display product ingredients and informations.
  • Interactive recipe batch calculator
E

Engineering

  • Heat transfer from the stove to the mixture during cooking.
  • Blending reduces particle size and increases viscosity.
  • Cooling causes a phase change from liquid to semi-solid gel.
M

Mathematics

  • Precise ratios of ingredients (sugar, gelatin, fruit mass) ensure optimal gelling and taste balance.
  • Calculating production cost per batch and material efficiency.
  • Determining exact peel-to-flesh ratio (1:4) for consistent consistency.

Step-by-Step Methodology

1

Preparation

Wash the dragon fruit. Separate skin and flesh.

→
2

Ratio Setting

Weigh the dragon fruit and set into a ¼ ratio.

↓
3

Steaming Skin

Steam the skin until soft to reduce bitterness.

←
4

Blending Puree

Blend the skin and flesh into a smooth puree.

↓
5

Prep Flavorings

Prepare the ginger and the lemon.

→
6

Boiling Ginger

Peel the ginger skin and boil the ginger.

↓
7

Adding Ginger Water

Add the ginger water to the puree for 10g.

←
8

Adding Lemon Juice

Squish the lemon and add 7.5ml - 15ml to each puree.

↓
9

Mixing Ingredients

Mix the puree with gelatin/agar powder, sugar, and cornstarch.

→
10

Cooking

Cook over low-medium heat while stirring until thick and smooth.

↓
11

Molding

Pour into a rectangular mold.

←
12

Setting

Cool at room temperature, then refrigerate until set.

↓
13

Cutting & Finishing

Cut into cubes or desired shapes.
