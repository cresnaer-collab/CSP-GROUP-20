Background

In SMA Pradita Dirgantara, students and campus residents mostly eat from rukan (kitchen/canteen). Most of the time, the kitchen does not utilize every single ingredient they have, causing ingredient waste to pile up and creating a problem that needs to be solved.

Contents & Bioactive Properties of Dragon Fruit

The Dragon fruit (Hylocereus spp.) contains bioactive phytochemical compounds with potent health-promoting properties.

Phenolic Acids & Flavonoids

Powerful polyphenolic compounds that scavenge free radicals and protect cellular structures from oxidative damage.

Antioxidant

Betalains

Natural red-violet pigments responsible for the vibrant color of dragon fruit peel. Exhibit strong anti-inflammatory activity.

Anti-inflammatory

Anthocyanins

Water-soluble pigments with demonstrated antimicrobial properties against common foodborne pathogens.

Anti-microbial

Functional Benefits

Combined bioactive profile supports cellular health, immune function, and provides natural food coloring without synthetic additives.

Interdisciplinary STEM Breakdown

PitaJell integrates all four STEM disciplines into a cohesive, real-world waste treatment project.

Biology

Examine nutrient content & antioxidant properties (phenolic acids, flavonoids, betalains, anthocyanins) in dragon fruit skin and flesh.

Chemistry

Make the substance gelatinous using natural pectin from dragon fruit skin waste combined with gelatin (100g), sugar (380g), cornstarch (½ tsp), and citric acid (½ tsp).

Physics

Analyze and ensure jelly texture is smooth via blending; find optimal temperature for setting across steaming (10 mins), stove cooking, and refrigeration.

Computer Science

Develop an interactive website showcasing materials, marketing/promotional content, and identifying optimal recipe sorting algorithms.

Step-by-Step Methodology

1

Cutting

Cut dragon fruit and carefully separate the flesh from the skin.

2

Peel Cleaning

Remove all green scales and bracts from the outer skin surface.

3

Steaming

Steam the cleaned dragon fruit skin for approximately 10 minutes to soften.

4

Blending

Blend the steamed skin together with the flesh at a 1:1 ratio until smooth.

5

Cooking

Cook blended mixture in a pot. Add 380g sugar, 100g gelatin, ½ tsp cornstarch, and ½ tsp citric acid. Stir continuously until thickened.

6

Molding

Pour the thickened mixture into a rectangular metal mold evenly.

7

Cooling & Setting

Refrigerate the mold overnight until the jelly is fully set and hardened.

8

Finishing

Unmold the jelly and coat the entire surface with granulated sugar for texture and presentation.
