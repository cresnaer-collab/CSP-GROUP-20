Background

Interdisciplinary STEM Framework

Integrating Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics for sustainable food innovation:

SCIENCE
  • Bioactive Compounds: Both flesh and skin provide antioxidants, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial agents (flavonoids, phenolic acids, betalains, anthocyanins).
  • Sustainability & Nutrition: Utilizing skin alongside flesh minimizes biological waste while maximizing dietary fiber.
  • Gel Formation: Gelatin molecules cross-link upon cooling to form a semi-solid gel matrix.
  • Solute & pH Dynamics: Dissolved sugars act as natural preservatives, while citric acid balances pH and product stability.
TECHNOLOGY
  • Interactive Platform: Develops an interactive website to display product ingredients, educational material, and targeted marketing/promotional content.
ENGINEERING
  • Heat Transfer: Thermal energy moves from stove to mixture during cooking.
  • Rheology & Viscosity: Mechanical blending creates uniform puree; viscosity increases as water evaporates.
  • Phase Change: Controlled cooling transitions solution from liquid to semi-solid gel.
MATHEMATICS
  • Ratio Calculations: Precise measurement ratios of sugar, citric acid, and gelatin relative to fruit mass ensure optimal gelling, taste balance, and shelf-life prediction.

Step-by-Step Instructions

STEP 1

Preparation

Wash fruit thoroughly. Separate pink skin & red flesh (1:1 ratio). Clean rough scales.

⤵
STEP 2

Steaming the Skin

Steam skin until softened to reduce bitterness and tough outer layer.

⤵
STEP 3

Blending

Blend softened skin and fresh flesh until completely smooth and uniform.

⤵
STEP 4

Combining Ingredients

Pour puree into pot. Add 100g gelatin, 380g sugar, ½ tsp cornstarch, ½ tsp citric acid. Mix thoroughly.

⤵
STEP 5

Cooking & Dissolving

Heat over low-to-medium heat. Stir continuously until sugar and gelatin fully dissolve.

⤵
STEP 6

Molding

Pour hot mixture evenly into rectangular metal mold or preferred container.

⤵
STEP 7

Setting

Cool at room temperature, then refrigerate until completely firm and set.

⤵
STEP 8

Cutting & Finishing

Unmold onto clean surface and cut into neat cubes or rectangular pieces.
