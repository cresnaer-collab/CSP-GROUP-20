📌 INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND
30–35% Dragon Fruit Peel Discarded as Organic Waste

In SMA Pradita Dirgantara, students and citizens mostly eat from Ruang Makan (Rukan). However, unused ingredients—especially dragon fruit peel—frequently end up as kitchen waste.

To solve this, we propose transforming dragon fruit peel waste using ginger and lemon to enhance flavor, taste, aroma, and health benefits, creating a delicious and healthy edible jelly (Nurhafsah et al., 2023).

✓ ANALYSIS

The data shows that 7.5 mL lemon with agar powder was the most preferred formulation, achieving the highest flavour (9.14) and texture (8.20) scores.

However, 15 mL lemon + gelatin had the highest aroma score (7.76). Overall, 7.5 mL lemon with agar powder provides the best sensory acceptance.

🎯 CONCLUSION

7.5 mL of lemon juice paired with agar powder serves as the optimal product formulation, outperforming all other variations.

It delivers the ideal balance of firmness, acidity, and sensory appeal. Agar powder is definitively preferred over gelatin for this blend.
