Background

In SMA Pradita Dirgantara, students and citizens mostly eat from rukan. But most of the time, the kitchen won't use every single ingredient they have, hence waste from ingredients begins to pile up, creating a problem needing to be solved.

Dragon fruit peel accounts for approximately 30-35% of the fruit's total weight and is commonly discarded as organic waste. (Nurhafsah et al., 2023)

STEM Framework

S

Science

"Why do the ingredients behave the way they do?"

Explains the properties of the ingredients and their interactions.

Dragon fruit peel contains vitamin C, antioxidants, and anthocyanins. Ginger contains 6-gingerol, a phenolic compound associated with its characteristic spicy and warming taste. Lemon provides vitamin C and acidity, while the gelling agent forms the jelly's characteristic texture during cooling.

T

Technology

"How can the concept be developed into a practical product?"

Supports the development, presentation, and accessibility of PitaJell.

A dedicated website presents the research, recipe, STEM concepts, data, and references, while a recipe calculator enables ingredient quantities to be adjusted according to the required batch size.

E

Engineering

"How can the production process be controlled?"

Focuses on the design and optimization of the production process.

Heating, blending, mixing, and cooling are controlled to achieve the desired texture and consistency. Ingredient ratios, temperature, and processing conditions are also considered during production.

M

Mathematics

"How can the product and its results be measured?"

Applied to ingredient ratios, production costs, and sensory evaluation data.

Feedback was collected from 15 participants through convenience sampling, with evaluations focusing on taste, texture, and color. The responses were analyzed using percentages, averages, and graphs.

Step-by-Step Methodology

1

Preparation

Wash the dragon fruit. Separate skin and flesh.

→
2

Ratio Setting

Weigh the dragon fruit and set into a ¼ ratio.

↓
3

Steaming Skin

Steam the skin until soft to reduce bitterness.

←
4

Blending Puree

Blend the skin and flesh into a smooth puree.

↓
5

Prep Flavorings

Prepare the ginger and the lemon.

→
6

Boiling Ginger

Peel the ginger skin and boil the ginger.

↓
7

Adding Ginger Water

Add the ginger water to the puree for 10g.

←
8

Adding Lemon Juice

Squish the lemon and add 7.5ml - 15ml to each puree.

↓
9

Mixing Ingredients

Mix the puree with gelatin/agar powder, sugar, and cornstarch.

→
10

Cooking

Cook over low-medium heat while stirring until thick and smooth.

↓
11

Molding

Pour into a rectangular mold.

←
12

Setting

Cool at room temperature, then refrigerate until set.

↓
13

Cutting & Finishing

Cut into cubes or desired shapes.
