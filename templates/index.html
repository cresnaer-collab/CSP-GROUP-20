Background

Contents of a Dragon Fruit & Health Benefits

The Dragon fruit (Hylocereus spp.) has lots of nutritious components rich in bioactive phytochemical compounds and health benefits:

Betalains

Nitrogen-containing water-soluble pigments responsible for the deep pink-violet hue of pitaya. Provide strong cellular protection and inflammation support.

Flavonoids

Secondary plant metabolites that scavenge free radicals and support vascular cell health. Excellent for cellular defense and radical neutralization.

Phenolic Acids

Potent hydroxylated compounds present in peel fiber that neutralize reactive oxygen species. Support oxidative balance and immunity boosting.

Anthocyanins

Vibrant plant pigments demonstrating natural antimicrobial and immunomodulatory activity. Active in microbial defense and immunity support.

STEM Framework

Cross-Disciplinary Integration in Food Innovation

S

Science

  • Gelatin forms a gel matrix when cooled, creating chewy texture.
  • Sugar acts as preservative and flavor enhancer.
  • Dragon fruit is rich in vital minerals such as calcium, magnesium, potassium, iron, phosphorus, and sodium.
  • Ginger is rich in various chemical constituents, including phenolic compounds, terpenes, polysaccharides, lipids, organic acids, and raw fibers.
T

Technology

  • Development of an interactive website to display product ingredients and educational information.
  • Digital weight scale for exact micro-gram measurements.
  • Precision temperature and pH control tools during cooking.
E

Engineering

  • Heat transfer from the stove to the mixture during cooking.
  • Blending reduces particle size and increases viscosity.
  • Cooling causes a phase change from liquid to semi-solid gel.
  • Designing an efficient waste-upcycling process flow.
M

Mathematics

  • Precise ratios of ingredients (sugar, gelatin, fruit mass) ensure optimal gelling and taste balance.
  • Calculating production cost per batch and material efficiency.
  • Determining exact peel-to-flesh ratio (1:4) for consistent consistency.

Step-by-Step Methodology

1

Preparation

Thoroughly wash the dragon fruit. Separate the pink skin and the red flesh, ensuring a 1:4 skin to flesh ratio (30g skin : 120g flesh). Clean the outer skin of any rough scales.

→
2

Steaming the Skin & Ginger

Place the dragon fruit skin and 15g ginger into the steamer. Steam until softened to reduce bitterness and unlock aromatics.

↓
3

Blending

Transfer the softened skin, fresh dragon fruit flesh, steamed ginger, and 50ml water into the blender. Blend until smooth and uniform.

←
4

Combining Ingredients

Pour the blended mixture into the pot. Add ½ tbsp gelatin, 3 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp cornstarch, and 15ml lemon juice. Stir thoroughly using a spatula.

↓
5

Cooking and Dissolving

Heat over low-to-medium heat. Stir continuously to prevent burning and ensure gelatin and sugar fully dissolve into a smooth, thick liquid.

→
6

Molding

Pour the hot mixture evenly into a rectangular metal mold or preferred container.

↓
7

Setting

Let it cool at room temperature, then refrigerate until completely firm and set.

←
8

Cutting and Finishing

Remove from the mold and place on a clean surface. Use a knife to cut into neat cubes or rectangular pieces.
